Notes before we begin

You showed up. That already says something.

  • Write first, edit never. The first thing out is usually the most honest.
  • Use the blank space. Draw, map, doodle. No rules.
  • The commitment page at the end of each session matters. Write it, sign it, mean it.
  • Return to earlier pages as the journey progresses. Things look different from further down the road.

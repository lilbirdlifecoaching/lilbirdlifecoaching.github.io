the person behind it

Hi. I'm Luke.
This started young.

I had forgotten who I was. I had gotten lost. It took a while for me to get put back together. Coaching was instrumental in helping me find my way back to who I know I am.

lil' bird is symbolic in my own journey. It reminds me of a time when there were no rocks in my backpack. When adventure was natural and inhibitions were no-where to be found.

I've built lil' bird to help you see yourself again: how you're wired, your story, the weird kaleidoscope that makes you you. While I don't take myself too seriously, I take coaching, and your highest good, very seriously.

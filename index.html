Hi. I'm Luke.
This started young.

"asked me to dream" "judgement doesn't exist" "uncanny ability to build trust"

I've spent years sitting with people in real transitions — the questions, the tension, the turning points. That shaped how I listen today: patient, direct, no agenda. Coaching is the container around that instinct.

lil' bird is what happens when you take that instinct and build a proper container around it: long sessions, good questions, and enough steadiness that you can hear yourself think again. Therapy and advice are different work; this is coaching.

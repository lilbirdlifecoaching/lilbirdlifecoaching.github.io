act one — the moment

Something has to
change.

The problem usually isn't a missing spreadsheet or a lack of discipline. It's living in the gap — between the life you built and the life that fits who you're becoming. Nothing is "wrong enough" to justify stopping. Nothing feels right enough to keep going. So you default to another week of performing fine.

Coaching isn't a pep talk. It's a pause long enough to tell yourself the truth, then choose a next step you can actually own.

✦

act two — how we work

Two hours.
Your story first.

Not a 45-minute pep talk. We slow down, map where you've been, name what's actually going on, and leave with one move you'll own — not a five-year fantasy plan.

01

Your story, mapped

Where you've been isn't baggage — it's data. We map what lit you up, what drained you, and what actually shaped you (not the résumé version).

02

Relationships and the voice beneath the noise

How you relate to yourself, to the people who matter, and to work — most "stuck" lives trace back here. We go there on purpose.

03

Tools, then one next move

Maps, inventories, prompts you can hold. Then one honest next move: small enough to do, true enough that you'll still respect yourself in a month. No five-year fantasy plan.

the format

Two hours. Story first. No slide deck.

Sessions vary person to person. The spine doesn't: slow down, go real, work with the you in the room — not the version you perform for everyone else.

what a session actually looks like

Two hours.
Story-first. All yours.

Whether it's your First Flight or you're deep in the Life Change curriculum, every session follows the same human structure: no script, no slide deck. Here's what you're walking into.

Opening — 20 min

Where are you right now?

We start with the actual moment you're in, not the polished version. What's weighing on you? What brought you here?

Story mapping — 40 min

Your story, mapped

We trace the arc of your life: the highs, the pivots, what actually shaped you. Where you've been is data, not baggage, and it usually holds a clue.

The Real Work — 45 min

Finding the actual issue

Most people come in with a surface problem. We go underneath — relationships, patterns, the voice beneath the noise. This is where things actually shift.

The Close — 15 min

One next move

We don't end with a five-year plan. We end with one true move: small, honest, and yours. Something you can actually do this week.

After the session

Tools you keep

Visual maps, relationship inventories, writing prompts: whatever we built together is yours. You leave with something tangible, not just something to think about.

clarity check

Coaching vs.
Therapy.

People ask this a lot. Here's the honest version.

Coaching (us)
Focused on where you're going
Works with your present and future
Goal: clarity, action, momentum
You're the expert on your own life
Not a clinical service
Great for transitions & growth
Therapy
Often explores where you've been
Works with past patterns & healing
Goal: healing, processing, insight
Clinician-guided treatment
Licensed mental health service
Great for trauma & mental health

Not sure which you need? That's a perfectly fine question to bring to the discovery call. We'll be straight with you.

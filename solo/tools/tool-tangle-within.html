The Tangle Within

Three vines that quietly shape what we do — fear, hiding, and proving. They're not character flaws. They're information. Name them honestly, and they lose some of their grip.

🌫️
The fear vine
"What am I most afraid of losing — or never having?"
🍃
The hiding vine
"What part of myself am I keeping hidden — and from whom?"
🔥
The proving vine
"What am I trying to prove — and to whom?"

Shadows & Sparks

Two things drive us: fear pushing from behind, and passion pulling from the front. Shadows are the fear-based motivators — the things you do to avoid a feeling. Sparks are the passion-based ones — the things you do because they genuinely matter. Neither is wrong. Both are information.

🌑
Shadows
Fear-based motivators

Things you do to avoid failure, judgment, rejection, or loss. What are you afraid people will think? What are you trying to prove?

✨
Sparks
Passion-based motivators

Things you do because they matter to you. Values, curiosities, causes. What lights you up even when it's hard?

One more question from lil' bird

Looking at your Shadows and Sparks — which list was easier to fill? What does that tell you about what's been driving you lately?

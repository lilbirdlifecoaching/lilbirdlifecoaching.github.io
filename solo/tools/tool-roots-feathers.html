Session 01 · Story

Roots & Feathers
Practice

Not everything you inherited is yours to keep. And not everything you want to become requires leaving the past behind. This practice helps you name what stays — and what you're ready to release.

🌿
Think about your story so far — the people, the seasons, the things you've carried. Some of what shaped you has become a genuine part of who you are. Some of it might be ready to let go of.

In the Roots column, name the things you want to keep — values, strengths, ways of being that truly belong to you. In the Feathers column, name the things you're ready to release — beliefs, patterns, expectations that were never really yours.
🌱 Roots
What I'm keeping

🪶 Feathers
What I'm releasing

Beliefs, patterns, or expectations that were inherited but may not be truly yours to carry.

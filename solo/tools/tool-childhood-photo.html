Younger you

A photo of yourself somewhere between four and eight years old. The version of you before you learned to perform. We'll keep them in your Full Flight Plan as a quiet reminder of where this all began.

Why this matters

At the end of the course, your illustrator turns this photo into a hand-drawn illustration that sits at the top of your Full Flight Plan — alongside the older you who's writing the plan now.

Choose one where you can see their eyes, if you can. Outdoor light is best. Doesn't have to be perfect.

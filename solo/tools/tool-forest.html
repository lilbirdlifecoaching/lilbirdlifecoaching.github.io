Session 06 · Relationships

Forest of Connections

Map the people in your life across four layers — from your closest inner circle to the more distant but meaningful connections. Be honest about where each relationship sits and what it's actually like.

🌳Canopy Trees
Closest relationships

The people you're most deeply connected to. They know the real you.

🌿Understory
Regular connections

People you see regularly and value — friends, colleagues, community.

🍂Forest Floor
Broader community

Acquaintances, neighbours, professional network. Important but not close.

🌲Lone Trees
Distant but meaningful

People you've lost touch with but still think about. Worth reconnecting?

Connection intention

Looking at your forest — which relationship most needs your attention right now? What's one specific thing you could do in the next week?

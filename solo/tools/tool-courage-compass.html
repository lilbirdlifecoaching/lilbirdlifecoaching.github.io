Courage Compass

When you're at a crossroads, four questions orient you. North is where you want to go. East is what's holding you back. South is what you're leaving behind. West is what you need for the journey.

↑ North

"Where do I want to go? What am I moving toward?"

→ East

"What's holding me back? What am I afraid of?"

↓ South

"What am I leaving behind? What needs to end?"

← West

"What do I need for the journey? Resources, support, courage?"

One brave step

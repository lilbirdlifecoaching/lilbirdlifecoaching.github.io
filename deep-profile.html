Your personality tells us who you are. The Life Canvas tells us the conditions you're operating in right now. Both matter — and together they tell a much more honest story.

Five areas. Five sliders. No right answers. This is just for you.

WHY Purpose — My place in the world

Do you wake up with a sense of direction — or does it feel like you're moving through fog? Think about your work, your calling, whatever gets you out of bed. When you imagine it, does it light something up or quietly drain you? Are you running toward something or away from it — or honestly, not sure?

WHERE Place — Where I've landed

Look around you — literally. Your city, your street, your home. Does it feel like yours, or like somewhere you're just passing through? If your soul needs trees, do you have trees? If it needs ocean, is it close? When you walk through your front door at the end of a hard day — does the space hold you, or add to the weight?

HOW Wellbeing — How I'm actually doing

Last one — and the most important. How are you doing? Not "fine." Actually. Are you sleeping? Moving? Does your body feel like an ally or something you're managing?

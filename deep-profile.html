Chapter 1 of 6 — The room you walk into

Let's start somewhere real.

Picture a room full of people you don't know well. A party. A work event. A community thing a friend dragged you to with promises of "it'll be fun, I swear."

Tell me honestly: what happens inside you in that first moment you walk in? Not what you do — what you feel.

Chapter 1 — Going a little deeper

That's honest. And already telling.

Is that how it's always been for you — or did something happen at some point that shaped the way you walk into rooms?

One more: when you leave that room at the end of the night, what do you usually feel? Relief? Energy? A mental replay of every slightly-awkward thing you said?


Chapter 2 of 6 — When things get hard

Everyone handles hard differently.

Think of a time something didn't go the way you hoped. Doesn't have to be dramatic — a plan fell apart, someone let you down, something you worked hard for just... didn't land.

Walk me through it. What happened, and more importantly — what did you do with it?


Chapter 2 — The underneath bit

Underneath all of that — what were you actually afraid of in that moment?

Not the surface stuff. The thing you maybe wouldn't say out loud to most people.

this is where it gets interesting.

When things go wrong — who do you tell? Or do you go quiet and figure it out alone first?


Chapter 3 of 6 — The people in your life

We learn enormous things about ourselves through other people.

Tell me about someone in your life — past or present — who you genuinely find hard to understand. Not someone you dislike necessarily. Just someone whose way of being in the world puzzles you.

What is it about them that you can't quite get?


Chapter 3 — What you give

Flip side: what do people tend to come to you for?

Not your job title. What do the people who genuinely know and love you bring to your door at 10pm when something's going on?

And is that something you love giving — or has it ever felt like a weight?


Chapter 4 of 6 — What you're made of

Now the good bit.

Describe a moment — could be totally ordinary — when you felt completely in your element. Like something clicked and you were doing exactly what you were made for.

What were you doing? And what made it feel that way?

What's something you do that other people seem genuinely impressed by — but to you feels so natural you almost don't count it as a real skill?

"oh that? anyone could do that." (they can't.)

Chapter 5 of 6 — The stuck places

This one takes honesty. You've got it.

What's the pattern you keep finding yourself in — even though part of you knows better?

The thing you do, or don't do, or say yes to when you meant no. The loop that shows up in different clothes but is somehow always the same loop.


Chapter 5 — What it costs

What does that pattern actually cost you? What do you lose, or miss, when it shows up?

If you had to trace it back — where do you think this pattern came from? When did you first notice it?

gut answer is usually the right one.

Chapter 6 of 6 — Where you're headed

Last chapter. The most important one.

If the version of you who had actually worked through all of this — the patterns, the stuck places, the things you've been carrying — if that person sent you a message right now, what do you think they'd say?

Don't overthink it. Just write what comes.

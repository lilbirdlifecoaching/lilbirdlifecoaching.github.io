Your personality tells us who you are. The Life Canvas tells us the conditions you're operating in right now. Both matter — and together they tell a much more honest story.

Five areas. Five sliders. No right answers. This is just for you.

WHY My place in the world

Do you wake up with a sense of direction — or does it feel like you're moving through fog? Think about your work, your calling, whatever gets you out of bed. When you imagine it, does it light something up or quietly drain you? Are you running toward something or away from it — or honestly, not sure?

WHO The people around me

Who actually makes up your daily life? Partners, family, friends, flatmates, colleagues. Are they filling you up or quietly emptying you? If you had to name the one relationship having the biggest impact on you right now — positive or negative — what's it doing to you?

WHERE Where I've landed

Look around you — literally. Your city, your street, your home. Does it feel like yours, or like somewhere you're just passing through? If your soul needs trees, do you have trees? If it needs ocean, is it close? When you walk through your front door at the end of a hard day — does the space hold you, or add to the weight?

WHAT What I have to work with

Be honest here — with yourself, not with us. How are you doing financially, materially? Not the polished version. Do you feel like you have enough, or is there a low-level hum of worry that follows you around? Are you building something, holding steady, or quietly sliding?

HOW How I'm actually doing

Last one — and the most important. How are you doing? Not "fine." Actually. Are you sleeping? Moving? Does your body feel like an ally or something you're managing? And mentally — are you okay, or are you carrying something heavy right now? If you fudge this answer, everything else in your profile will be slightly off. So be real. Are you trending up or spiraling down?

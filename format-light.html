what a session actually looks like

Two hours.
Story-first. All yours.

Whether it’s your First Flight or you’re deep in the Life Change curriculum, every session follows the same human structure — no script, no slide deck.

Opening — 20 min

Where are you right now?

We start by getting honest about the actual moment you’re in — not the polished version, the real one. What’s weighing on you? What brought you here?

The Glow Map — 40 min

Your story, mapped

We trace the arc of your life — the highs, the pivots, the moments that shaped you. Your past isn’t baggage. It’s data. And it usually has the answer.

The Real Work — 45 min

Finding the actual issue

Most people come in with a surface problem. We go underneath. This is where the relational intelligence work happens — the stuff that changes things.

The Close — 15 min

Your next brave step

We don’t end with a five-year plan. We end with one true move — something small, honest, and yours. Something you can actually do this week.

After the session

Tools you keep

Visual maps, relationship inventories, writing prompts — whatever we built together is yours. You leave with something tangible. Not just something to think about.

clarity check

Coaching vs.
Therapy.

People ask this a lot. Here’s the honest version.

Coaching (us)
Focused on where you’re going
Works with your present and future
Goal: clarity, action, momentum
You’re the expert on your own life
Not a clinical service
Great for transitions & growth
Therapy
Often explores where you’ve been
Works with past patterns & healing
Goal: healing, processing, insight
Clinician-guided treatment
Licensed mental health service
Great for trauma & mental health

Not sure which you need? That’s a perfectly fine question to bring to the discovery call. We’ll be straight with you.

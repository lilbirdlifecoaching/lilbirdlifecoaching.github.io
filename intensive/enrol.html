Life Change Intensive — Coaching Agreement (draft)

Parties. This agreement is between you (“Client”) and Luke Haythorpe / lil' bird coaching (“Coach”).

Programme. The Life Change Intensive includes seven (7) in-person coaching sessions, approximately two hours each, following the Roots & Wings curriculum. Sessions are booked by you via the scheduling link provided after enrolment.

Fee & payment. The total programme fee is shown at checkout on lilbird.life. Payment is due in full before your first session is booked. Fees are processed securely by Stripe.

Scheduling. You will receive a Calendly link to book all seven sessions. Rescheduling is welcome with reasonable notice; repeated no-shows may affect remaining sessions at the Coach's discretion.

Confidentiality. What you share in sessions stays between us, except where disclosure is required by law or there is risk of harm to you or others.

Your rights. Coaching is not therapy, medical, or legal advice. You may pause or discontinue at any time; fees for unused sessions may be refunded or credited only as agreed in writing with the Coach.

Materials. You receive access to the Roots & Wings workbook and your Nest dashboard for the duration of the programme.

Agreement. By typing your full legal name below and completing payment, you confirm you have read and accept this agreement.
